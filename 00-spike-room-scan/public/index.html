Room scan spike

Enters passthrough AR and reads what the headset knows about this room: its planes (floor, walls, ceiling, door and window frames) and meshes (furniture boxes, the global mesh), drawn over the real room. Trigger (or pinch) saves a scan to the laptop. Grip asks the headset to run Space Setup. A scan is also saved automatically a few seconds after the room appears.
