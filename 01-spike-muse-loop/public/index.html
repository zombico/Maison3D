Maison3D loop

Talk to the agent from inside the room. In AR, hold the trigger (or pinch and hold), say what you want, and let go: what you pointed at goes with it. Your words are transcribed on the headset, the agent changes the cube, and its answer is shown and read aloud, without leaving AR.

First, on this page: 1. Allow mic. 2. Load voice model (about 40 MB the first time, then cached). 3. Try Hold to talk. Then Enter AR.
