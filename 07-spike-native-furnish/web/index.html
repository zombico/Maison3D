Furnish a room by talking to it

You're standing in an empty 20 by 15 ft room. Say or type what you want; click the floor, a wall or a piece first to say "here" or "this". Drag to look around, scroll to move in. Drag a piece to move it (Q and E turn it, Delete removes it). Shift-drag over the floor or a wall to highlight an area: "a sofa that fits here".

On a Quest, open this page in the browser and press Enter VR: hold the grip to talk, pull the trigger to point, or pull and drag it across a wall or the floor to mark an area (B does too). Hold it on a piece to carry it (the thumbstick turns it). Push the thumbstick forward to step to where you point, left or right to turn.
